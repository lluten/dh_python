Start your Python exercise

From opening your computer to running your own code. Follow these steps at your own pace.

Your first steps

  1. Choose your environment. Open Setup to choose a browser-based notebook or a local installation.
  2. Run your first example. Follow Workflow to create a virtual environment and run your first script.
  3. Start your exercise. Use the same write, save, and run routine for your exercise.

As the course progresses

Lecture 01a: start with setup and your first run.

Lectures 06a–b: learn where to put your exercise data.

Lecture 10b: follow package setup for spaCy and NLTK when your exercise needs them.
